# V1-v2 Interpretation Boundary

## Claims supported

- The historical paper-facing v1 results are exactly reproducible from frozen model-level outcomes.
- Same-response rescoring quantifies sensitivity to repaired root/evaluator definitions while preserving the historical subset surface.
- The repaired v2 estimate is the primary confirmatory answer and shows substantial confirmed susceptibility for Qwen, but not uniform confirmation across both model configurations.
- Numerical changes may be described as estimate shifts after integrity repair.

## Claims not supported

- No pure or causal candidate-surface repair effect is identified.
- No candidate-set confounding amount is estimated.
- The shift cannot be attributed solely to holder repair, because root semantics and evaluation validity also change in the harmonized replay.
- V1-to-v2 differences cannot isolate candidate surface, evaluator repair, fresh execution, output budget, response controls, time, or residual remote-model nondeterminism.
- A small positive DeepSeek point estimate must not be described as absence of a semantic shortcut; it did not satisfy the frozen confirmation criterion.
